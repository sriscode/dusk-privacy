Why Dusk

Not a note-taker.
A thinking partner.

✦

AI asks the questions

After you write, on-device AI generates one thoughtful follow-up question. Not advice — curiosity. It's the nudge that turns a dump into a reflection.

🔒

Completely private

All AI runs on your device via Apple Foundation Models. Your journal never touches a server. No account required to write a single word.

📈

Patterns over time

Pro users unlock deep AI insights across all their entries — mood trends, recurring themes, and observations you wouldn't notice yourself.

🌙

Built for evenings

Two-minute UX. Dark by design. A single daily ritual that replaces ten minutes of scrolling with ten minutes that actually matter.

🛡️

Your thoughts stay
on your phone

Dusk uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models for all real-time AI. No servers see your words. No data is collected. No account means nothing to breach.
